### Bug fixes

- The switch from Autodarts to HomeDarts finishes even when an entry of the old integration is left that HomeDarts can't take over, such as that of a board that an early version set up and that hasn't answered since. Before, the media stayed in the old folders, the cards kept their old names and the old integration stayed installed for good, without a word about why. The entry stays as it is, and a warning in the log names it. A switch that an earlier version left unfinished this way finishes at the next start of Home Assistant, and so does one where you delete the board of the old integration instead of taking it over.
