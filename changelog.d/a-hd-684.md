### Bug fixes

- A training session no longer reports a visit of more than three darts, such as the 360 of six triple 20s that a missed takeout merges into one visit, as its highest visit. Like the 100+, 140+ and 180 visits, the personal best and the weekly report, the sensor *Training highest visit*, the event `session_ended`, the history of the sessions and the calendar entry now keep the highest real visit; the merged darts still count for the darts, points and average.
