### Bug fixes

- The length of a set is called by what it means: *Legs to win a set* on the new game screen, in the actions and in the number *Practice legs to win a set*, where it said *Legs per set*, and the header of the scoreboard reads *3 legs to win a set* instead of *3 legs per set*. A match with two legs to win a set can end 2 : 1, which the old name hid. The entity ID stays `number.<board>_practice_legs_per_set`, so your automations and dashboards keep working.

### Features

- The highlight gallery no longer grows until the disk is full: after it saves a photo, it keeps the newest 1000 photos, each with its clip, and deletes older ones. *Highlights kept* of the highlight photo blueprint, or `keep` of `homedarts.save_highlight`, changes the number, and `0` keeps every photo. Only photos and clips that HomeDarts named go, never a file you copied there, and a folder of your own is never pruned. `homedarts.save_highlight` also saves at most 120 photos in an hour, so that an automation that loops cannot fill the disk.
