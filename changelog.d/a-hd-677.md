### Features

- The summary of a finished match opens again: tap a match under *Recent matches* of the players card to see its checkout rate, 180s, best leg and the other numbers of the scoreboard's summary, long after the next game started. The match history now keeps the last 50 matches instead of 20; the card lists the latest ten and the others behind *All 50 matches*. Matches stored before this version have no summary and stay plain rows.
