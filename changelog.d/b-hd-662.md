### Bug fixes

- A dartboard without Autodarts gets its Autodarts board also when you once ignored the discovery of that board. Before, *Reconfigure* stopped with "already configured" for an address you entered, and *Search for boards* didn't list the board at all.
- The new game screen takes two names for one player exactly when HomeDarts does: `WEIẞ` and `Weiß` are one player, as `WEISS` and `Weiß` already were, and a name of emoji is cut to twenty characters without breaking its last emoji. Before, such a pair got into one lineup without a warning, and the rename dialog didn't say that the two players would be merged.
- Merging two players who both played a finished tournament leaves the tournament as it was played. Before, both entrants took the new name, so its table, bracket and podium showed one name twice.
