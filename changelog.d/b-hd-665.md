### Bug fixes

- The cards stay readable in a browser older than they are made for: their status and text colours fall back to the plain colours of the theme, and the *Most hit* list of the training card keeps its columns. The documentation now names the browsers the cards need (Safari 16.4, Chrome, Edge and the Android System WebView 111, Firefox 121 or newer) and what an older wall tablet shows.
- Texts you see call the integration by its name, HomeDarts for Autodarts: the repair notice of a board answer the integration does not understand, in every language, and the highlight photo blueprint, whose gallery the media browser shows under HomeDarts.
