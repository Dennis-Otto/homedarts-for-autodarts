### Bug fixes

- The new game screen of the scoreboard waits for the start: if HomeDarts refuses it, the screen stays open with every player and shows why, and detection stays as it was. A new tournament that HomeDarts refuses, such as one whose players don't fit into the groups, no longer stops the tournament being played. A typed name goes in as HomeDarts keeps it, without curly brackets, percent or number signs (*Tom #2* plays as *Tom 2*), and only once in any upper and lower case (*WEISS* is *Weiß*).
