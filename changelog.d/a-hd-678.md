### Features

- The training card has an editor of your own training plans: as an administrator, tap *Training plans* in its header to give each plan a name, the weekdays on which it is the plan of the day, and its steps, each a training game with a number of darts or visits or played to its end. *Save* keeps them for the board at once, and the options of the board show the same list in YAML as before.
- When own plans are not valid, the options of the board and the editor now say which plan and which step is at fault and why, such as an unknown training game, more than one limit or a name that another plan has, instead of one message for the whole list.
