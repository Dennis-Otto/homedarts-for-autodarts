### Bug fixes

- A highlight photo or clip that `homedarts.save_highlight` or the highlight photo blueprint saves into a folder of `www` now gets a random part at the end of its name, as an export there does, and its `/local/` address as `photo_url` and `clip_url`. Home Assistant serves `www` without a login, and before, the name followed from the moment, the player and the score, so anyone who reached Home Assistant could guess it. The description of the folder and the documentation now say that files in `www` are public. Photos in the gallery keep their names.
