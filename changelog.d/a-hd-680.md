### Features

- A new blueprint, *HomeDarts: training reminder*, reminds you at a time you choose, by default at 7 pm, while today's daily goal is still open or while your training streak would end tonight because no dart was thrown today. You choose the weekdays and the streak from which it reminds you; its ready-made sentence, such as "10 darts left to your daily goal of 10. Throw a dart today to keep your streak of 2 days.", is in the language of Home Assistant, and the *Darts today* and *Training streak* sensors hold it in their new `reminder` attribute.
