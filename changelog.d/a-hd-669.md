### Bug fixes

- A dartboard without Autodarts that you delete and add again is offered its backup now. Such a board gets a new board ID with every setup, so the repair never found the backup written when it was deleted. Now a dartboard without Autodarts with no backup of its own shows *A backup of … is available for …* with the newest backup of a deleted one, named after the board it belonged to, and once you restore it, the backups of the deleted board become those of the new one, so no other board is offered them again.
