### Bug fixes

- A board of the integration under its old name, Autodarts, that you pick in the search or by its address is now taken over, as when Home Assistant discovers it: before, HomeDarts set it up anew beside the old one, which then could no longer be taken over, and the switch to HomeDarts never finished. If that happened to you, a repair names the new board: confirm it, and HomeDarts deletes the new board, with a backup of what it recorded, and takes the old one over with its entities, statistics and training.
